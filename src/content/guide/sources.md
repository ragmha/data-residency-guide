## What we could not verify

No public source answered these questions at the review date. Ask GitHub or Microsoft directly and keep the answers in writing:

- Whether customer-managed encryption keys are available for GHE.com.
- Whether any commitment limits GHE.com operations or support to EU-based personnel.
- Whether Microsoft sovereignty programmes, such as the EU Data Boundary or Microsoft's sovereign cloud offerings, formally cover GitHub.
- Retention periods for Copilot chat data on GHE.com.
- Which DPA version a Microsoft purchase agreement references. The Microsoft privacy and security terms could not be read at review time.
